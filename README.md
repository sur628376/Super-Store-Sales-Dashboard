# Super-Store-Sales-Dashboard
# 📊 Super Store Sales Dashboard | Power BI

An interactive **Power BI Sales Analytics Dashboard** designed to analyze Super Store sales performance, profitability, quantity, regional performance, customer segments, payment modes, shipping modes, product categories, and monthly trends.

The dashboard transforms raw sales data into meaningful business insights using **Power BI, Power Query, DAX, and interactive visualizations**.

---

## 🚀 Project Overview

The objective of this project is to build an interactive business intelligence dashboard that helps understand:

- Overall sales performance
- Profitability and quantity sold
- Regional sales performance
- Monthly sales and profit trends
- Payment mode distribution
- Shipping mode performance
- Product category performance
- Product sub-category performance
- Customer segment contribution

The dashboard provides an easy-to-understand view of business performance and helps identify important sales and profitability patterns.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze overall sales and profit performance.
- Identify high-performing regions.
- Understand monthly sales and profit trends.
- Compare different payment modes.
- Analyze shipping mode contribution.
- Identify top-performing product categories.
- Identify important product sub-categories.
- Compare sales across customer segments.
- Create an interactive dashboard for business decision-making.

# 📊 Dashboard Analysis

## 1. Sales by Payment Mode

The dashboard analyzes sales across different payment methods:

- Online
- COD
- Cards

Online payment contributes a significant portion of total sales, showing the importance of digital payment channels in the business.

---

## 2. Sales by Region

Regional analysis helps identify differences in sales performance across:

- Central
- East
- South
- West

The regional visualization can be used to identify high-performing markets and regions where additional sales strategies may be required.

---

## 3. Sales by Customer Segment

The dashboard compares sales across customer segments such as:

- Consumer
- Corporate
- Home Office

This helps understand which customer groups contribute significantly to overall sales.

---

## 4. Sales by Month

The monthly sales chart shows changes in sales throughout the year.

### Key Observation

Sales show noticeable fluctuations across different months, with stronger performance toward the later part of the year.

December shows one of the strongest sales levels in the dashboard, indicating increased business activity toward year-end.

---

## 5. Profit by Month

The profit trend provides a month-by-month view of profitability.

### Key Observation

Profit varies throughout the year and shows stronger performance during several later months.

December records one of the highest profit levels, indicating strong year-end business performance.

---

## 6. Sales by Ship Mode

The dashboard compares:

- Standard Class
- Second Class
- First Class
- Same Day

### Key Insight

Standard Class contributes the largest portion of sales among the available shipping modes.

This indicates that customers use standard shipping considerably more than faster shipping options.

---

## 7. Sales by Category

The dashboard analyzes three major product categories:

- Office Supplies
- Technology
- Furniture

### Key Insight

Office Supplies generates the highest sales among the three categories, followed by Technology and Furniture.

Based on the dashboard analysis:

### 🔹 1. Strong Overall Sales
